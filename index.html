Our Story

Nearly Five Decades
In the Making

The Book Market has been part of the Crest Hill and Joliet community longer than most of its customers have been alive. Here's how we got here.

The Doors

The Book Market opened in Joliet at Jefferson & Reed Street — a modest storefront with big ambitions. From day one, the mission is simple: put great books into the hands of everyday readers at prices anyone can afford.

1977

[First Big Move]

After 30 years The Book Market relocated to 2368 Plainfield Road in Crest Hill and doubled its store size due to closing of the West Side Shopping Center

2007

Second Relocation to Our Permanent Home

More space; more books. What started as a modest storefront has become a fixture of the Crest Hill community, with thousands of loyal customers who've made it part of their routine. The Book Market made its second and final relocation right across the street to 2365 Plainfield Road! This move doubled our space again and serves as our home today and will continue to be our space forever!

2015

The Book Market Partners with WriteOn Joliet

With our newly expanded space The Book Market welcomed a partnership with local authors who begasn dispalying their work and scheduing regular signings for our reading loving community.

2016

A Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite

The Book Market earns Nextdoor's Neighborhood Favorite distinction — voted by the very neighbors it has served for decades. Recognition across nine neighborhoods in the greater Joliet and Chicagoland area, a testament to over 45 years of community trust.

2023

Still Here. Still Growing.

Nearly five decades after opening day, The Book Market remains independently owned and operated — by the original owner. We may have moved locally over the years but we maintained the same commitment. New titles arrive daily, new readers discover us for the first time, and longtime customers keep coming back. The story isn't finished yet.

How It Works

Purchase, Trade & Give Back

We've built a simple, fair system that keeps books circulating and money in your pocket. Whether you're shopping or trading — there's a place for you here.

01

Shop at 40% Off

Most books on our shelves are priced at 40% off the publisher's price. A full bookshelf has never cost so little.

02

Receive Store Credit

Looking to trade? Bring your books in and we'll evaluate them on the spot. Receive store credit to put toward your next great read.

03

Trade Your Books

Bring in books you no longer need and trade them in — it's a simple, no-fuss process that lets you use your credit the same day.

04

Out of Print Books Available

Have books you've finished with? Drop them off. Every donation extends the life of a great read and keeps prices low for our entire community.
