What We Host

How We Bring the
Community Together

The Book Market has been a gathering place for readers and writers since 1977. In partnership with WriteOn Joliet and the Joliet Public Library, here's the kind of events you can expect from us throughout the year.

✍️

Local Author Signings

Meet the authors who live and write in our community. Browse, purchase, and get your copy signed — these events are intimate, personal, and always free and open to the public.

Monthly throughout the year
🎉

Anthology Release Parties

Celebrate new anthologies from WriteOn Joliet — featuring multiple local authors, live book signings, homemade light refreshments, and readings from the collection.

Annual release events
🤝

Meet-the-Author Evenings

A more casual sit-down with a featured author — hear about their process, inspiration, and what they're working on next. Every author who has signed here has been warm and approachable.

Ongoing
🖊️

WriteOn Joliet Partnership

We're a proud partner of WriteOn Joliet. Their members — journalists, novelists, poets, screenwriters, bloggers — sign books here regularly as part of our ongoing community collaboration.

Regular monthly signings
📖

Book Launch Events

Debut a new title right here at the store. Authors read from their work, answer questions from the community, and sign first copies fresh off the press.

Several times per year
❤️

Charity & Benefit Signings

Some of our events go beyond books. Local authors have used their signings at The Book Market to raise funds for causes like the United Way of Will County right here in the store.

Special occasions
Looking Back

Past Events

A selection of real events from The Book Market's history — verified author signings, anthology release parties, and community gatherings documented in local press.

Author Signing March 2024

Bill O'Connell — Joliet True Crime

Joliet author Bill O'Connell visited the store for a signing of the second edition of his book documenting a fascinating local Joliet crime case — updated with new material. Noted for being engaging and approachable with fans and first-time readers alike.

✓ Free & open to the public · Sat., March 23, 2:00–5:00 PM
Author Signing May 2021

Lindsay Lake Signs Debut Novel Flower Power

Joliet-area author and WriteOn Joliet member Lindsay Lake signed copies of her debut novel Flower Power — an adventurous story inspired by the 50th anniversary of 1969 and written during the pandemic. Her first novel event at the store.

✓ Debut novel event · Free & open to the public · Sat., May 1, 1:00–4:00 PM
Charity Signing November 2020

Tom Hernandez — Signing for United Way

Plainfield author and WriteOn Joliet co-leader Tom Hernandez signed copies of his poetry, essays, and fiction at The Book Market, donating all author proceeds to the United Way of Will County. Special discounts offered for multiple purchases.

✓ All author proceeds donated to United Way of Will County
Author Signing June 2020

Colleen Robbins — The Daraga Series

WriteOn Joliet member Colleen Robbins — an Odyssey Fantasy Writer's Workshop alumna whose work spans literary fiction, science fiction, fantasy, and horror — signed copies of her Daraga series. Her work has appeared in numerous anthologies and genre magazines.

✓ Free & open to the public · Sat., June 20, 12:00–3:00 PM
Author Signing January 2020

Denise Baran-Unland — The BryonySeries

WriteOn Joliet co-founder and Herald-News features editor Denise Baran-Unland signed copies of her BryonySeries vampire novels and children's Cornell Dyer chapter books. Baran-Unland helped establish the ongoing Book Market–WriteOn Joliet partnership.

✓ Free & open to the public · Sat., Jan. 25, 3:00–5:00 PM
Anthology Release November 2019

WriteOn Joliet 3rd Anthology: Write Where We Are

Twenty Chicagoland writers celebrated the release of WriteOn Joliet's third annual anthology at The Book Market with book signings, readings, and homemade refreshments. This party built on the tradition started here in 2018 with the group's second anthology release.

✓ 20 contributors · Free community event · Fri., Nov. 15, 6:00–8:30 PM
Also On Record

Other verified signings at The Book Market include: Holly Coop (poet, WriteOn Joliet — July 2019), Dale Hansen (author and community mentor — August 2019), Jennifer Russ (author of The Heart's Bone and Whitewallsville — May 2019), and the WriteOn Joliet 2nd Anthology release party — the event in November 2018 that launched the formal Book Market–WriteOn Joliet partnership under owner Jan Staley.
