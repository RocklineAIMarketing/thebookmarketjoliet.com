We offer store credit for books. We do not offer cash, just store credit. Bring them in, we'll evaluate them on the spot — no appointment needed for smaller collections. Before You Bring Them In

What We Do & Don't Accept

We'd love to take every book that comes through the door — but to keep our shelves fresh and valuable for shoppers, we do have to be selective. Here's what we're generally looking for.

✅

We Accept

  • Fiction & non-fiction novels (paperback & hardcover with dust cover)
  • Children's & young adult books
  • Classic literature, poetry & required school readings
  • Graphic novels
  • Psychology, self help & philosophy
  • Audiobooks in CD and MP3 format
  • Religious texts, Bibles, devotionals & religious fiction
  • Mystery, thriller, sci-fi, horror, romantica & fantasy
  • Romance & historical fiction
  • Sports, biography, travel & memoir
  • Westerns & war
  • Large print & Spanish
  • Books in clean, readable condition
🚫

We Don't Accept

  • Books with heavy water damage or mold
  • Books with missing pages, dust covers, or torn covers
  • Heavily written-in or highlighted copies
  • Encyclopedias & outdated reference sets
  • Reader's Digest condensed books
  • Magazines
  • Textbooks & preowned library copies
  • Books with strong odors or staining

Not sure if your books qualify? Don't stress — just bring them in and we'll take a look together. We'd rather you bring something uncertain than leave a good book at home. When in doubt, call us at (815) 744-4240 and we're happy to talk it through.
